Quick project I made to impress my friends at the D&D table. They will need to solve this puzzle to open a hatch in the sewers to invade a forgotten hobgoblin dungeon.
Completely inspired by the first level of [NandGame](https://nandgame.com/).

You can play the game at [itch.io](https://rochoso.itch.io/crystal-shard-gate)!

# Update
Ran the session and the players loved the puzzle! They managed to figure it out in 50 minutes and it took a lot of collaborative thinking. It was very fun!
